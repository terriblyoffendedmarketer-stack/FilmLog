Recommendations are NOT limited to the watchlist. Watchlist is one source among many. Claude should use its full intelligence (taste model, journal, hall of fame, anti-signals) to suggest any film — including ones Prashil has never heard of.

**Why:** The product is a personalized magazine issue. Imagine coming home from work, picking up a magazine where the writer knows you deeply — your opinions, your journal entries, your taste profile. That writer recommends 2-3 films with real editorial prose. It's a Claude chat that happens to be about film.

**How to apply:**
- Tonight page = editorial issue generator. Not a form, not a filter.
- Watchlist candidates get priority but the system can and should suggest films outside the watchlist when taste-matched
- The "issue" format is sacred — short editorial, a few films, each with a personal "why you" reason
- No standardized rating system. Stars are personal feeling (5 = loved, 4 = great but one thing held it back). Never compare ratings across films.
- Hall of Fame films should have their own dedicated section — always visible, always clear
- Films with strong opinions (positive OR negative) should be tagged/surfaced — they're the highest-value taste signal
