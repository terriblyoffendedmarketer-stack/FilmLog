Prashil is not a coder. He thinks about tools from a user/product perspective — progress bars, clear feedback, sensible defaults.

He wants Filmlog to be a persistent knowledge system for his film taste, not just a tool. The intelligence should persist across sessions and be portable (GitHub).

He has film notes scattered across Notion, Google Keep, and a published Substack. He wants to dump these in bulk, not enter them one at a time.

He values being told what's happening and how long things will take. Silent processes with no progress feedback frustrate him.
